Regression Models

1 published project

Predicting continuous values, comparing against baseline rules, and evaluating errors on future periods.

Online Retail — Purchase Forecasting

Context:

Forecasting each customer's gross purchase value over the next 30 days, using 90 days of transaction history to support revenue planning.

Approach:

A CRISP-DM workflow with temporal feature preparation, comparison of 23 regression configurations, and evaluation on a period held out until model freeze. Governed development with AI-agent assistance, human review, and approval within the workflow.

Result:

On the 2,461-customer test set, the historical rule outperformed regression on RMSE (£927.77 vs. £1,127.79). The recommendation retains the simpler method, supported by a documented model, tests, and evidence.

Python · Scikit-learn · Tweedie Regression · CRISP-DM · Temporal Validation · ML Governance

Clustering Models

1 published project

SellerScope — Marketplace Seller Intelligence

Context:

Segmenting Olist marketplace sellers to guide commercial and logistics investigation using real public data.

Approach:

CRISP-DM, seven dimensions, 15 K-Means, GMM and Ward configurations, subsampling stability and later-period evaluation with a frozen model.

Result:

Two profiles among 592 sellers, silhouette 0.166 and mean ARI 0.871. Among 372 common sellers, 86.6% keep their group in the later period. Descriptive segmentation with overlap and documented action hypotheses.

Time Series

1 published project

DemandScope — Sales Forecasting & Planning

Context:

28-day Walmart/M5 store-department sales forecasting to support commercial and replenishment planning.

Approach:

CRISP-DM, 70 series, eight candidates, four rolling origins, horizon-safe lags and a final test after model freeze.

Result:

HistGradientBoosting achieved 9.99% WAPE versus 12.05% for the baseline: 17.1% relative error reduction in the historical test. Nominal 90% intervals covered 91.4% of observations. No realized inventory savings are claimed.

Cabe no Bolso

Context:

A proactive conversational AI product designed to support customers when their credit card bill no longer fits their monthly budget. The solution identifies financial stress signals before the due date and turns the diagnosis into a simple, contextual, action-oriented journey.

Approach:

Using financial behavior and bill projections, the agent contextualizes the situation and presents only credit alternatives already deemed eligible by deterministic systems, such as changing the due date, overdraft options, or credit offers with installments that fit the customer's budget.

Key Differentiator:

Clearly separates financial decisioning from conversation: credit rules and systems determine amounts, limits, and eligibility, while AI explains options, costs, and consequences in simple language, preserving consent, traceability, and human control.
