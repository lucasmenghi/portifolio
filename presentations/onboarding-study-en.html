01 / Product decision

How can identity-verification abandonment be reduced?

The journey allows users to upload a document now or postpone it. The case investigates where users leave, which behaviors are associated with approval and how to test a clearer experience.

Proposed decision: test guidance and visual hierarchy on the document screen while preserving a recovery option. The public case demonstrates the method with generated data and discloses no internal systems or metrics.

02 / Analytical foundation

One journey connected across three tables

100,000 users in a synthetic January–June 2025 cohort, 504,686 events and 14,794 support contacts. The primary unit of analysis is the user, preventing event volume from inflating conversion.

04 / Behavior and conversion

Immediate upload is associated with higher approval

Approval among users choosing immediate upload: 35.00% (10,424 of 29,783). Among users postponing: 4.10% (2,876 of 70,217). The descriptive difference is 30.90 percentage points.

The 95% Wald interval for the difference is 30.34 to 31.47 p.p., restricted to this synthetic sample. Choice was not randomized and the generator embeds this pattern. Document readiness and session quality affect selection. This is not a causal screen effect.

05 / Hypotheses and prioritization

Investigate the cause before removing the option

Hypotheses to validate: unavailable documents, unclear guidance, camera/upload failure and an intention to finish later. Prioritize guidance, error feedback and recovery with Product, Design and Engineering.

DMAIC application: define the friction, measure the funnel, analyze segments, test a change and control operations. UX hypotheses are investigation proposals, not root causes established by the CSVs.

06 / Proposed experiment

Test the experience with user-level allocation

Primary sizing metric: immediate-upload choice. Baseline 29.783%, target 33.357%, relative MDE 12% (+3.574 p.p.). At two-sided alpha 5% and power 80%, approximately 2,654 users are required per arm.

Real-pilot proposal: persistent 50/50 assignment at eligible entry, a predefined assessment window and intention-to-treat analysis. Track approval, support, technical failures and verification quality as secondary metrics and guardrails. Recalculate sample size with actual eligible traffic.

07 / Simulation consistency

The existing A/B gain is built into the simulation

The notebook converts 11% of users who would postpone to immediate upload. With this cohort mix, that implies approximately 37.51% immediate upload: an expected relative gain of about 25.93%, above the 12% sizing MDE.

The A/B simulation demonstrates an analysis workflow. Its results do not show that an interface works. To simulate the 12% target, the postponing-user conversion parameter would be about 5.09% under the same rule. Measure the actual effect and check allocation imbalance in a real pilot.

08 / Financial scenarios

Value depends on the effect and assumptions

At +12% relative immediate upload: approximately 1,104 additional approvals and 352 fewer users contacting support per month, assuming 100,000 new users/month. Modeled annual net benefit: 73.2 thousand monetary units.

09 / Control and operations

Define decision criteria before the pilot

Before: validate instrumentation, eligibility and baseline. During: monitor allocation, upload errors, completion and support by segment. After: assess the effect with confidence intervals, cost and verification quality.

Gradual rollout depends on criteria agreed with the business. No production reduction in support demand or incremental approvals has been observed in this repository. The professional onboarding result mentioned in the CV is a separate account.

10 / Synthesis

A recommendation that can be tested

Prioritize document submission and test a clearer experience. The case demonstrates how to connect funnel diagnosis, statistical interpretation and economic scenarios to support a decision.

Core limits: synthetic data, association embedded by the generator, simulated experiment and scenario-based economics. Next step: validate the problem and instrumentation with authorized real data before deciding rollout.
